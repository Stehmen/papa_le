The pâtissier

Trained as a pastry chef,
built for the morning shift

Aleksei Solovev graduated in cooking and confectionery, then spent fifteen years in professional kitchens — from sous-chef to head chef — building menus, training brigades, and obsessing over the last gram of sugar.

Papa Le is his pâtisserie: a small sunlit room where the croissants get twenty-seven layers, the honey cake follows his grandmother's recipe, and the first bake leaves the oven at five in the morning.
